# 🎓 Nashama Student Portal (البوابة الرقمية للطلاب)

A comprehensive, all-in-one web platform designed to support students at **Al-Zaytoonah University of Jordan (ZUJ)**. Built to centralize essential academic resources, campus navigation, transportation schedules, and student services into a single, easy-to-use digital hub.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* **📚 Academic Library & Summaries:** Access course summaries, university requirement materials, and remedial/placement exam models (نماذج امتحانات استدراكي).
* **🧮 GPA Calculator:** An interactive tool allowing students to accurately calculate both their Semester GPA (المعدل الفصلي) and Cumulative GPA (المعدل التراكمي).
* **🗺️ Interactive Campus Map & Buildings Guide:** Detailed directory of university buildings, halls, and facilities to help students—especially freshmen (السنافر)—navigate the campus effortlessly.
* **🚌 Transportation & Bus Routes:** Complete schedules and route guides for university buses, along with dedicated transportation coordination groups.
* **💬 Student Communities & Book Exchange:** Direct access to college-specific groups, freshmen communities, and a student book-exchange network.
* **📖 Student Handbook (دليل الطالب):** Clear guide outlining student rights, academic regulations, and responsibilities.
* **🔗 Quick University Links:** One-click access to essential platforms including Microsoft Teams, the University Portal, and the E-Learning System.
* **📩 Complaints & Suggestions Box:** A dedicated feedback channel for students to submit inquiries, issues, or suggestions.
* **🤝 About Nashama List (قائمة النشامى):** An introductory section highlighting the Nashama student coalition at ZUJ, showcasing a gallery of campus events, activities, and official social media channels.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* **Backend:** PHP
* **Frontend:** HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript
* **Media & Assets:** Integrated event galleries, videos, and interactive campus visuals

---

## 💡 Project Impact

This platform was engineered to eliminate the hassle of scattered information by providing students with a reliable, centralized, and mobile-friendly portal that enhances their daily university experience.
